Professional Biography

After completing his medical graduation from Rawalpindi Medical College, Rawalpindi, Dr. Farooq pursued postgraduate training in General Medicine at Mayo Hospital, Lahore, one of Pakistan’s largest tertiary-care hospitals, affiliated with King Edward Medical University, one of the oldest and most prestigious medical institutions in South Asia.

He subsequently earned his fellowship from the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan (CPSP) in Internal Medicine. Since completing his postgraduate training, Dr. Farooq has been actively involved in providing consultant-level medical services across emergency, outpatient, and inpatient departments.

His clinical responsibilities include the assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and ongoing management of patients presenting with a broad spectrum of acute and chronic medical conditions.

In his current leadership role as Head of the Department of Medicine at DHQ Hospital, Hafizabad, he contributes to the delivery of medical services, clinical decision-making, and the coordination of patient care. His work reflects a commitment to professional excellence, evidence-based medical practice, and improving patient outcomes.

In addition to his clinical responsibilities, Dr. Farooq is an approved supervisor of the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan (CPSP) for fellowship training in Medicine. Through his supervisory role, he contributes to the professional development of postgraduate trainees, helping guide the next generation of physicians in clinical practice, medical knowledge, and professional competence.
