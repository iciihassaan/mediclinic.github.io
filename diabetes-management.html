What is Diabetes Management?

Diabetes is a chronic condition that affects how your body turns food into energy. At Dr. Farooq Mediclinic, we believe that effective diabetes management goes far beyond just checking blood sugar levels. It's a partnership between you and your doctor to create a personalized plan that fits your lifestyle, goals, and medical needs.

Our approach combines the latest evidence-based medicine with a deep understanding of the daily challenges faced by people living with diabetes. Whether you've just been diagnosed or have been managing diabetes for years, we're here to help you optimize your health and reduce the risk of complications.

What Our Program Includes

  • Comprehensive Assessment: Detailed evaluation of your blood sugar patterns, medication history, and overall health to create a baseline.
  • Blood Sugar Monitoring: Guidance on using glucose meters and continuous glucose monitors (CGM) effectively, and interpreting your results.
  • Medication Management: Personalized medication plans, including insulin therapy, oral medications, and the latest GLP-1 options. We adjust as needed based on your response.
  • Nutrition & Lifestyle Counseling: Practical, culturally-sensitive dietary advice and exercise recommendations that fit your life.
  • Complication Screening: Regular monitoring for kidney health, eye health, nerve function, and cardiovascular risk factors.
  • Ongoing Support & Education: Continuous access to resources, and regular follow-up appointments to keep you on track.

Why Choose Dr. Farooq for Diabetes Care?

Dr. Farooq has extensive experience managing complex diabetes cases, including patients with co-existing conditions like hypertension, heart disease, and kidney issues. Our clinic offers:

  • Personalized Care Plans: No two people with diabetes are the same — your treatment plan is uniquely yours.
  • Accessibility: Conveniently located at DHQ Hospital, Hafizabad, with flexible appointment times and telehealth options available.
  • Focus on Prevention: We work hard to help you prevent complications before they start.
  • Compassionate Approach: We understand the emotional toll of diabetes. You'll always be treated with respect and empathy.
